# Phone recording, maps and replay

Mode: Operate. Keep the user-pinned simple monotone interface, few words and one free sporty icon family. Use native Android Material structure, neutral day/night roles and pinned Tabler outline icons. Real satellite imagery keeps its colors. Version 0.5 adds Spanish as the personal-build default and English as the other app language; preserve native font scaling and accessible names in both.

The idle Record tab is an activity selector for Run, Walk or Hike and a Start button, without a map. Remember the choice and persist it in the session through recording, recovery, history and export. Old untyped sessions stay Run; do not guess from speed. Once Android permissions/settings are satisfied, Start takes one tap.

Active, paused and recovered sessions hide the app bar/tabs and fill the content area with a map. A bottom island inset 16dp contains state/GPS status, equal Distance · km / Time / Avg /km columns and recording actions. Portrait adds climbed/descended elevation estimates. Landscape moves the island lower left, capped at 340dp or 45% width; information can scroll while actions remain reachable. Activities/options float at the top. Android system bars stay visible.

Cover the initial navigation map with a location-loading state until a usable fix exists, keeping relevant permission/GPS status available. Do not flash the world map before centering. The north-up map follows at zoom 18, centered in the unobscured area. A Tabler arrow turns with GPS movement; the initial marker is neutral until direction is reliable. This is not a compass. Stops retain course; gaps/segments reset its baseline. Pan/pinch disengages follow; location restores it or explains that a fix is unavailable. Interpolate safe consecutive live fixes with the camera, with a short display delay; never extrapolate or mutate stored points. Stop animation when paused/backgrounded and do not glide across gaps or implausible jumps.

Recording offers Pause; paused/recovered sessions offer Resume and Finish. Discard remains in options with confirmation. Active actions are at least 60dp high, or 48dp in landscape, with 16dp radii; the island has 24dp corners and 3dp native elevation. Nominal 26sp live values fit 16–26sp as needed, with 12sp labels and a 16sp state heading. Start remains at least 64dp high. The shield/options expose Recording protection and Android settings. Keep permission, map, storage and recovery errors actionable.

Saved replay also fills the content area behind a compact bottom island, lower left in landscape. Back/options and a 48dp download control float above it. Distance, speed and climbed/descended elevation lead the island; Play/Pause, 1×/5×/10×/30×/60×, seeking, time and pace stay within reach. Default speed is 1×. Tap the statistics or Details to open a bottom sheet with totals, climb/descent, a 160dp relative elevation-change profile and splits. Raw GPS altitude/range are secondary. The sheet pauses playback. Replay pauses in the background, preserves position/speed through recreation, and skips pauses and GPS gaps over 15 seconds. No MP4 is produced.

Elevation estimates use GPS height changes and a 3-metre deadband, without corrected terrain heights or grade percentages. Preserve missing-height and segment gaps in the profile, whose zero is the first available GPS height. Use — for unknown values. Keep calculation details in on-demand information rather than filling the island with prose. Static documentation previews contain no invented measured route or elevation profile.

Export GPX replaces Strava. Save to Downloads on Android 10+, with a document picker on Android 8/9 or after an automatic-save failure. File generation/writing stays off the UI thread. Show the actual saved name and explicit Copy name action. Readable names use localized activity, distance and local start date/time; no street guesses or UUID labels. Export opens no browser, connects no account and publishes nothing. Share file is a separate Android chooser action. Source recordings survive cancelled or failed exports.

The ongoing notification shows distance, average pace, an active chronometer and expanded GPS status in the app language. Pause is available while recording; Resume/Finish while paused or recovered. Actions validate identity/state, and notification tap opens recording. Lock-screen presentation depends on Android and user settings, including silent-notification visibility. Samsung may show icons until expanded; Now bar support is not promised and the app does not register as music playback.

Satellite imagery uses EOX globally and detailed IGN/PNOA in Spain, with place/road labels. Route/marker overlays are local. Fit/follow and the map-credit strip use 48dp targets. Credits remain visible around both islands and expose source/coverage/privacy information. Providers receive visible-area requests and network addresses, not raw recordings or timestamps. Tile failure must not stop capture; whole-region offline downloads are not implemented. Local recovery copies are not cloud backup, and a powered-off phone cannot record.
