# Phone recording, maps and replay

Mode: Operate. The user pinned a simple monotone interface with few words and one sporty free icon set, then requested satellite maps, replay, more statistics, a full-screen live map with a bottom island, heading arrow and useful lock-screen controls. Use native Android Material structure, neutral day/night roles and pinned Tabler outline icons. Controls stay monochrome; real satellite imagery intentionally retains its colors. The short state says Ready, Recording, Paused or Run recovered.

Start takes one tap once required Android permissions/settings are satisfied. During active, paused and recovered sessions, the app bar/tabs give way to a map filling the content area. A bottom island inset 16dp contains state/GPS status, three equal Distance · km / Time / Avg /km columns and recording actions. Portrait adds GPS speed and altitude. Landscape moves the island to the lower left, capped at 340dp or 45% width; information can scroll while actions remain reachable. Runs/options controls float at the top. System status/navigation bars remain Android-owned.

The north-up map follows at zoom 18, centering the current position within the unobscured area around the island, controls and credits. A pinned Tabler navigation arrow turns with accepted GPS movement; a neutral marker remains until movement establishes reliable direction. This is not a phone compass. Stops retain the last known course, while GPS gaps and new segments reset its baseline. Pan/pinch disengages follow; the location control restores close following, and the route control fits the track. Map rendering remains separate from recording.

Recording offers Pause; paused/recovered runs offer Resume and Finish. Discard stays in the options menu with confirmation. Active-island controls are at least 60dp high, or 48dp in landscape, and use 16dp radii; the island has 24dp corners and 3dp elevation. Active values are nominally 26sp, fitting 16–26sp as needed, with 12sp labels and a 16sp state heading. Use native font scaling, paired night colors and system Back. The ready screen retains its 240dp map (120dp below 500dp screen height) and at least 64dp Start action.

A shield action or the active-run options menu exposes Recording protection, Android app settings and notification settings. Preserve permission, map, storage and recovery messages; short copy must not hide failures. Icons support labels or have accessible names. A saved run keeps the labeled Strava action in its toolbar so statistics do not bury it.

The ongoing notification shows distance, average pace, active chronometer and expanded GPS status. Pause is available during recording; Resume and Finish appear while paused/recovered, with a frozen timer. Actions validate run identity and state, and tapping the notification opens the recording screen. Lock-screen presentation depends on Android and user settings, including separate silent-notification visibility on some phones. Samsung may show icons until expanded; Now bar support is not promised, and the app does not register as a music player.

Satellite imagery uses EOX global coverage and high-resolution IGN/PNOA coverage in Spain with place/road labels. The route and marker are local overlays. Fit/follow controls have accessible names; a 48dp map-credit strip remains above or beside the island and opens source/coverage details. Tile providers receive the requested visible areas and network address, not raw recordings or timestamps. A tile failure must leave GPS recording and local calculations unaffected; no downloaded offline regions are promised.

Saved-run replay uses a 300dp map, labeled Play/Pause, 1×/5×/10×/30×/60× speed and a seek bar; default speed is 1×. Below 500dp screen height, use a 160dp map and place distance beside time/pace. Replay pauses when backgrounded and skips pauses and GPS gaps over 15 seconds. Show speed, pace, altitude, replayed distance, climbed/descended estimates and a 144dp altitude chart, followed by run totals and splits. Preserve missing-altitude gaps from original fixes; use — for unknown values. Replay is an in-app view, not exported video.

Strava sharing stays subscription-free: save directly to Downloads on Android 10+, then open the browser uploader. Android 8/9 and failed automatic saves use a file picker. Keep the actual saved filename available. Selecting the file, reviewing visibility and publishing are still manual on Strava; no connected/uploaded state is inferred. Local recovery is separate from continuous capture: a powered-off phone cannot record, and internal copies are not cloud backup.
