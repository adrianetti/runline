Stop animation when paused/backgrounded and do not glide across gaps or implausible jumps.

When follow is disengaged, project the native marker from its displayed GPS coordinate every frame and on camera movement; rotate its bearing relative to the camera. Do not route position updates through asynchronous GeoJSON marker layers. Hide off-viewport positions rather than clamping them to an edge. Pause/disposal stops frame callbacks.
