Runline downloads nearby data, interpolates heights and smooths grades. These derived estimates are not endorsed by the source agencies. Source resolutions, dates and vertical references vary. Global Mercator tiles exclude the polar caps; water, bridges, trees, buildings and recent changes may be represented incorrectly for a runner. Missing data remains unknown. The app code's MIT license does not replace these data terms.
